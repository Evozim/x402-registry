## Review and follow-up

1. Read the proposal and existing review conversation. Link related issues and duplicate proposals so one project is not counted twice; preserve the applicant's original submission and evidence.
2. Check the official project reference, working public website and documentation, logo, category and networks. Do not infer project ownership from a wallet alone.
3. For a paid product, distinguish a seller API from a payment facilitator. Check the stated route and method, payment terms, network, asset and decimals against public documentation. Separate operator-test settlements from customer activity. Do not initiate paid calls as part of a listing review without a separately authorized payment workflow.
4. Record the evidence checked and any remaining question in the review. Distinguish requested changes, approval and merge. A proposal merge is not proof that a website listing or indexer has gone live.
5. Confirm publication separately against the live directory. Keep reported wallet activity, indexed settlement evidence, coverage and time windows distinct; missing activity data is not zero usage.

For applicant requirements, use the [listing guide](README.md) and [proposal template](.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md). Do not publish credentials or private applicant details in a review.
